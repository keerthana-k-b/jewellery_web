Shipping & Returns

1. SECURE SHIPPING & LOGISTICS

Aurelia partners exclusively with specialized high-value carriers (such as Sequel and Malca-Amit) to execute delivery logistics. All orders are packed in secure, tamper-evident boxes and fully insured during transit.

  • Shipping Cost: Free standard shipping for all jewelry purchases globally.
  • Delivery Window: In-stock designs arrive in 3-5 business days. Custom ring sizes, engravings, or bespoke casting modifications require 7-10 business days for delivery.
  • Verification: A direct physical signature by an adult is required upon receipt. Parcels cannot be deposited in post boxes or left without physical verification signatures.

2. RETURN POLICY & LAB FEES

We offer a 30-day return window from the delivery receipt date. Returned items must remain in pristine, unworn condition with security labels intact, packaged inside their original presentation box and documentation folders.

  • Certificate Returns: All diamond grading certificates (issued by GIA or IGI) must be returned intact. Due to evaluation processes, missing laboratory certificates are subject to a **$250 re-certification surcharge**.
  • Exclusions: Custom engraved jewelry, custom cast silhouettes, and personalized resizing orders are final sales and ineligible for standard returns.

3. EXCHANGE & RESIZING GUIDELINES

Aurelia provides one complimentary ring resizing service within 60 days of purchase (excluding bespoke sizing designs). Standard designs can be exchanged for alternative models of equal or greater value, subject to a stock availability inspection. Sizing adjustments requiring gold addition or setting rebuilds will be quoted by our atelier experts prior to restoration.
