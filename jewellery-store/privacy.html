Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JUNE 15, 2026

At Aurelia Fine Jewellery ("Aurelia," "we," "us," or "our"), client data privacy is foundational to our brand relationship. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, secure, and disclose your personal details when you interact with our boutique storefronts, digital checkout systems, or client assistance hotlines.

1. INFORMATION COLLECTION METHODS

To complete luxury jewelry transactions, custom engraving services, or secure delivery checks, we collect the following classes of data:

  • Identity Information: Full name, telephone coordinates, email address, physical delivery addresses, and billing credentials.
  • Payment details: Credit/debit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing balances processed strictly via PCI-DSS compliant secure vaults.
  • Customization preferences: Ring sizing diameters, specific custom text engravings, metal preferences, and purchase histories.

2. INTENDED DATA USES

Your collected details are processed strictly for the following operational workflows:

  • Processing transactions and executing fully-insured logistics.
  • Managing GIA/IGI diamond database registration references.
  • Validating location codes for shipping eligibility checks.
  • Coordinating bespoke design appointments or responding to support queries.

3. DATA SHARING & SECURITY

Aurelia does not sell, rent, or lease customer data profiles to marketing aggregates. We disclose identity details exclusively to logistical partners (e.g., Sequel, Malca-Amit) and merchant banking networks to finalize transactions.

All digital connections utilize Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption, and card transactions conform to standard PCI-DSS specifications. Physical database servers are managed under restricted authorization locks.

4. USER ACCESS RIGHTS

Clients retain rights to request access to their registered accounts, rectify erroneous details, or demand complete deletion of profiles under standard protection codes (including GDPR or CCPA requirements where applicable). To file a request, contact our privacy officer at concierge@aurelia.com.
