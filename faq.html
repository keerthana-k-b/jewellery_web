Frequently Asked Questions

Discover information on shipping tracking, diamond quality audits, ring measurements, and care recommendations.

1. SHIPPING & DELIVERY LOGISTICS

HOW LONG DOES DELIVERY TAKE AND IS SHIPPING INSURED? expand_more

Every order contains free fully-insured courier transit via specialty shipping firms (such as Sequel or Malca-Amit). In-stock items ship within 24-48 business hours and arrive in 3-5 business days. Custom engraving orders require an additional 5-7 business days of preparation.

CAN I REDIRECT THE PARCEL DURING TRANSIT? expand_more

For security and fraud prevention, we require a direct hand-delivery signature upon arrival at the shipping address provided during checkout. Addresses cannot be redirected once the high-value transit package has left our secure holding vaults.

2. RETURNS & EXCHANGES POLICY

WHAT IS YOUR STANDARD RETURN WINDOW? expand_more

We offer a 30-day return window for unworn items in pristine condition, accompanied by all original certification tags, packaging structures, and grading documents. Custom engraved rings and bespoke orders cannot be returned.

ARE THERE CHARGES FOR PRE-PAID RETURNING PACKAGES? expand_more

We provide pre-paid insured shipping labels for standard size exchanges or quality returns. Please contact our concierge desk to arrange a courier pickup slot. Returns missing GIA diamond certificates are subject to a $250 laboratory re-evaluation fee.

3. PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS & DIAMONDS

WHAT CERTIFICATIONS ACCOMPANY THE DIAMONDS? expand_more

All our solitaire stones above 0.3 carats are audited and stamped by the Gemological Institute of America (GIA) or the International Gemological Institute (IGI). The physical certificate packet is shipped along with the final jewelry box.

DO YOU SOURCING LABORATORY OR NATURAL STONES? expand_more

Aurelia specializes strictly in natural mined diamonds of high color and clarity grades (D-H color, IF-VS2 clarity). Our materials are ethically sourced and verified conflict-free through standard Kimberley Process declarations.

4. JEWELLERY CARE & MAINTENANCE

HOW SHOULD I CLEAN MY DIAMONDS AT HOME? expand_more

Clean gold and diamond surfaces using lukewarm water and a drop of pH-neutral dish soap. Gently scrub using a soft-bristled brush (like a baby toothbrush) and dry thoroughly using a clean microfibre cloth. Avoid harsh household cleaning agents.

HOW OFTEN SHOULD I SCHEDULE RESTORATION & RESEALS? expand_more

We recommend booking an expert maintenance inspection at an Aurelia showroom once every 12 months. Our artisans will inspect prong alignments, tighten settings, and polish gold surfaces to restore original luster.
