A CENTURY OF ELEGANCE

Elevating the Human Spirit Through the Art of Fine Jewellery

Since 1924, Aurelia has stood as a beacon of luxury craftsmanship, carving legacy statements out of gold, platinum, and diamonds. Our philosophy combines age-old techniques with modern minimalism.

OUR INCEPTION

The Aurelia Legacy

Founded in the historic quarters of Antwerp by master artisan Charles Aurelius, our house originally served private royalty collectors across Western Europe. Over generations, the studio migrated its headquarters to craft legacy showrooms in capital cities globally, establishing Charles’s design tenets—unmatched symmetry, hidden details, and heavy solid weights.

Today, Aurelia is managed by third-generation designer-owners, expanding Charles’s classical shapes with modern, airy frameworks that trap light and feel virtually weightless on the wearer, yet retain structural permanence.

1924 — THE APPRENTICE’S BENCH

Charles Aurelius opens a small gem-cutting studio in Antwerp, prioritizing high-clarity rough diamonds.

1958 — GLOBAL CERTIFICATION LEAD

Aurelia becomes one of the early adopters of GIA grading methods, guaranteeing standard carat audits.

2002 — ETHICAL TRANSITION

The company transitions exclusively to conflict-free Kimberley Process diamonds and fair-trade mine sourcing.
